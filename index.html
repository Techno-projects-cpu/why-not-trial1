Paper & Ink — the quiet one

Editorial minimalism: warm paper, real typography, hairline rules instead of boxes. Almost no chrome. The design gets out of the way and lets the addresses, the phone number and the facts do the selling. Built to load on a 4G phone in a village and to look composed on a 6-inch screen and a 27-inch monitor alike.
