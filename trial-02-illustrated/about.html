About us

Started with one shop and a question.

On 27 February 1994 Mr. Jujjavarapu Venkata Rayudu opened an LML showroom in Tanuku and called it Whynot. Thirty-two years later it is 25 showrooms and 300 people — still run by the same family.

The story

Sticky notes from the last 32 years

1994

An LML showroom in Tanuku

A commerce graduate and junior lawyer from Kanuru village starts his first venture, at a time when good products rarely reached semi-urban Andhra Pradesh.

1996

A Bajaj dealership

Two-wheelers at scale — and the reputation for after-sales service the electronics business still runs on.

2006

Automobiles to electronics

The shift to consumer electronics and home appliances, and expansion across the East and West Godavari districts into Visakhapatnam district.

2025

WHYNOT Electronics Pvt Ltd

From 27 February 2025 the company incorporates formally — structured governance, stronger systems, deeper brand partnerships, a digital-first plan.

Today

25 showrooms, 300 people

A shop in nearly every town of the two Godavari districts, more than 2.5 million customers served, and the family still at the desk.

Next

Same shop, online too

The stated ambition is 50+ branches and an omnichannel experience — same prices, same people, same service, wherever the customer starts.

The founder

“Nothing is impossible.”

Mr. Jujjavarapu Venkata Rayudu grew up in an agricultural family in Kanuru, West Godavari. He studied commerce, then law, and started out as a junior lawyer before business pulled him in.

His instinct was to go where bigger retailers did not: the tier-3 and tier-4 towns. That decision built the network — and the barrier around it.
