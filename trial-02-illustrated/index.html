The name is the question

Why not…

Four questions WHYNOT has answered the same way since an LML showroom in Tanuku in 1994.

why not…

…put it in your town?

Twenty-five showrooms in 23 towns, because “drive two hours to the city” is not service. Most of them opened before anyone else thought the town was big enough.

why not…

…pick up the phone after the sale?

Installation, service and follow-up come from the branch you bought at, not from a call centre that has never seen your street.

why not…

…give the honest price first time?

Ask us today's price and you get the number we can actually sell at. EMI and exchange details upfront, no theatre.

why not…

…still be here in ten years?

Thirty-two years, two generations of the same family, 300 people from the towns they serve. Your neighbour is the shop.
