The name is the question

Why not better, closer, and for longer?

Four questions WHYNOT has been answering the same way since 1994 — through an LML showroom, a Bajaj dealership, and 32 years of electronics.

01

Why not bring it to your street?

WHYNOT opened in tier-3 and tier-4 towns when big retail only counted the metros. Today 25 showrooms sit in the towns people actually live in — not two hours away.

02

Why not pick up the phone after the sale?

The relationship starts at the counter, not on the invoice. Installation, service and follow-up come from the same town you bought in.

03

Why not price it honestly, first time?

A passion-oriented business, in the company's own words — not a profit-oriented one. You get the best price we can offer, without the theatre.

04

Why not think in decades?

32 years, two generations, one family, 300 people. When your customers are your neighbours, you don't get to disappear in year three.
