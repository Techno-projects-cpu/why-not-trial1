About us

Thirty-two years, one family, one district at a time.

WHYNOT began on 27 February 1994 as an LML showroom in Tanuku. Three businesses, two generations and 25 showrooms later, it is still run by the family that started it.

History

A business built in places other retailers skipped.

1994

An LML showroom in Tanuku.

Mr. Jujjavarapu Venkata Rayudu — a commerce graduate and junior lawyer from Kanuru village — opens his first venture on 27 February 1994, at a time when mobility was aspirational and quality products rarely reached semi-urban Andhra Pradesh.

1996

A Bajaj dealership.

Two years in, WHYNOT takes on two-wheelers at scale, building the reputation for after-sales reliability that the electronics business still runs on.

2006

Automobiles to electronics.

The business shifts fully to consumer electronics and home appliances, and expands across the East and West Godavari districts and into Visakhapatnam district.

2025

WHYNOT Electronics Pvt Ltd.

Effective 27 February 2025 the company incorporates formally — structured governance, stronger systems, deeper brand partnerships and a digital-first plan.

Today

25 showrooms. 300 people.

A showroom in nearly every town of the two Godavari districts, more than 2.5 million customers served, and the same family at the desk.

Next

A seamless, omnichannel WHYNOT.

The stated ambition is to keep growing the shop floor and match it online — same prices, same people, same service, wherever the customer starts.

The founder

“Nothing is impossible.”

Mr. Jujjavarapu Venkata Rayudu grew up in an agricultural family in Kanuru, West Godavari. He studied commerce, then law, and began his career as a junior lawyer before business pulled him in. In 1994 he put his name on a shop and called it Whynot — a question rather than a statement.

His instinct was to go where the competition did not: the tier-3 and tier-4 towns that bigger retailers considered too small. That decision built a network, and a barrier, across the Godavari districts.

Leadership today: Mr. Jujjavarapu Venkata Rayudu (Managing Director), Mrs. Jujjavarapu Sridevi, Mrs. Jujjavarapu Mounika and Mr. Muttavarapu Siddharth Venkatram (Directors).
